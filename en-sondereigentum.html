Management for your apartment.

Do you let an apartment in Berlin and want help with the day-to-day responsibilities? We look after your tenants, prepare service-charge statements and coordinate repairs, even if you live elsewhere. This service, known as Sondereigentumsverwaltung (SEV), works alongside the building's owners' association (WEG) management.

What you can expect from us

  • One point of contact for managing your rented apartment
  • Local support, even if you live outside Berlin
  • A point of contact for tenants and their concerns
  • Service-charge statements for your apartment
  • Coordination of repairs in your apartment
  • An overview of rental income, costs and upcoming works
  • Digital access to your apartment's documents
  • Coordination with the WEG manager on building matters
