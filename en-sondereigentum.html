Individual unit management (SEV)

Single apartments, reliably managed.

For capital investors, remote owners and institutional ownership structures with individual condominium units. We take over the management of your unit (Sondereigentumsverwaltung, SEV), with tenant management, audit-proof reporting and digital document access, in coordination with the WEG management of the building.

Mandates Rental buildings WEG Individual units

What you can expect from us

  • Management of individually owned condominium units (SEV)
  • Ideal for capital investors and remote owners
  • Tenant management and service-charge statement for the unit
  • Reporting on rental income, costs and works
  • Digital access to all documents of your apartment
  • Coordination with the WEG management of the building
  • Audit-proof documentation and auditable processes
  • Clear interfaces for asset management and owner representation across multiple units
