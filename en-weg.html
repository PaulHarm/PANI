WEG management in Berlin.

For owners' associations (WEG) that expect structured, transparent and legally sound management: clear resolutions, sound accounting and reliable day-to-day execution, so your association stays able to act and the value of your property is preserved.

What your association can expect

  • Structured owner meetings and consistent implementation of resolutions
  • Transparent service-charge accounting and clear reserve management
  • Forward-looking maintenance planning to preserve value
  • Legally sound management under current German condominium law (WEG)
  • A dedicated contact for the advisory board and owners
  • Digital access to resolutions, statements and documents

How we take on your WEG

01

Assessment

Association, resolutions, contracts, reserves and open issues at a glance.

02

Resolution and termination

All the change requires is a resolution of the owners. Under section 26 (3) of the German Condominium Act (WEG), an existing manager can be removed at any time; the current contract then ends no later than six months afterwards.

03

Orderly handover

A clean transfer of the management records, the annual budget (Wirtschaftsplan) and clearly documented processes.

04

Management & reporting

Ongoing support with transparent reporting and reliable execution.

Included in the mandate

  • Preparation and chairing of owners' meetings
  • Implementation and documentation of resolutions
  • Annual budget (Wirtschaftsplan) and service-charge statements
  • Management and planning of the maintenance reserve
  • Maintenance and works coordination
  • Coordination of tradespeople and service providers
  • Support for the advisory board and owners
  • Owner report and digital document archive

Every mandate includes structured reporting and document access. See Reporting.
