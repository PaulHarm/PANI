Construction support with oversight.

Roof, facade, pipework or stairwell: building works affect the budget, the residents and everyday life in the building. We keep them together, from the initial survey through tendering and contract award to coordinating everyone involved and final acceptance.

Discuss construction support
Construction site · not a managed property
Renovation supportConstruction support

What you can expect from us

  • One fixed contact for owners, contractors and specialists
  • Comparable quotes instead of a single offer
  • Costs and schedules kept in view throughout
  • Timely information for residents and tenants
  • Alignment with resolutions and reserves for owners’ associations
  • Complete records of award, acceptance and warranty

How a project runs

01

Survey

Together with specialists we establish what the building needs and record scope, urgency and budget.

02

Tendering and award

We obtain comparable quotes, prepare the decision and award the work once you approve.

03

Coordination

During the works we coordinate dates, access and information between contractors, specialists and residents.

04

Acceptance and records

We accompany the acceptance, follow up open defects and keep warranty periods in view.

What construction support covers

  • Survey with qualified specialists
  • Obtaining and comparing quotes
  • Preparing resolutions for the owners’ meeting
  • Awarding contracts once the owners approve
  • Scheduling with contractors and residents
  • Cost control against the approved budget
  • Accompanying acceptance and following up defects
  • Filing all documents and monitoring warranty periods

Design, structural engineering and site management are carried out by qualified architects and specialist planners. We coordinate everyone involved and represent your interests as the owner. For energy measures see renovation support.

Two worlds. One standard.

PANI is run personally by its two founders: analytical discipline shaped by investment banking, and management craft from a family that has looked after buildings for generations. For your building works this means reliable numbers and careful execution in the details.
