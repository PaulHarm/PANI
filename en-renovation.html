Energy renovation.

PANI coordinates the renovation process. Qualified specialists handle planning and technical checks. As the owner, you decide which measures to take, which contracts to award and what to spend.

How we support your renovation.

01

Building & goals

We gather existing documents, clarify your goals and coordinate the building assessment with specialists.

02

Planning & funding

Specialists assess potential measures and funding options. We agree the next steps with you and everyone involved.

03

Tenders & contracts

We support tendering and coordinate the review of quotations. You decide on the budget and which contracts to award.

04

Works & acceptance

We coordinate the parties, schedules and preparation for acceptance. Qualified specialists carry out the technical checks.

Potential in existing buildings

From high running costs to a future-ready building.

We see energy renovation as an opportunity for sustainable property management, not an obligation. When it makes sense for your building, we coordinate the process from the initial assessment through funding and tendering to acceptance, together with qualified specialists.

We identify opportunities openly; the decision remains yours. Better efficiency can reduce running costs and CO₂ emissions and support the long-term value of your property.
