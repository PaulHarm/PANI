Home/Enquiry

Make an enquiry.

A few short questions about your property, then your contact details. At the end, an email draft opens with your answers for you to check and send yourself. Nothing is sent automatically.

Step 1 of 6

What can we manage for you?
01

What can we manage for you?

Choose whatever fits best. If you are unsure, pick the last card and we will clarify it when we talk.

How many units does the property have?
02

How many units does the property have?

A unit is an apartment or a commercial space. An estimate is enough; for several buildings, give the total.

What is your role in the association?
03

What is your role in the association?

We only ask this for an owners’ association. It tells us who we are speaking to and who takes the decision.

How is the property managed today?
04

How is the property managed today?

This tells us whether a handover is due and how urgent it is. This enquiry goes to us only.

Which topics are coming up?
05

Which topics are coming up?

You can select several. You can also skip this step.

Where is the property?
06

Where is the property?

PANI looks after buildings across Berlin. The location helps us plan visits on site. You can give the address in the next step, but you do not have to.

How can we reach you?
07

How can we reach you?

Above the form you will see your answers as a summary. You can adjust any of them there using "Change". After clicking "Prepare email", a draft opens in your email app with all your details.

Your answers

You chose "Not sure yet" or "Something else". Please describe your request briefly in the message field.

No obligation and confidential. Nothing is transmitted automatically: your details only open an email draft in your own email app addressed to info@pani-hv.de, which you can check and change before sending. Read more in our privacy notice.

Prefer to get in touch directly? Email info@pani-hv.de or call 030 23125 150.
