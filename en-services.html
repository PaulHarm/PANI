How we start together.

Understand first. Decide next. We organise the starting point and agree the next steps with you.

  1. 01

    Understand the property

    We discuss the building, ownership structure, records and the issues that matter right now.

  2. 02

    Visit the property

    We visit your property in person to gain our own overview of the building.

  3. 03

    Define the scope

    You receive the right scope for management, reporting, maintenance or further projects.

  4. 04

    Organise the handover

    We coordinate decisions, notice periods, records and the handover with your current manager.

  5. 05

    Manage reliably

    During the mandate, tasks, decisions and open points remain visible and moving forward.
