How can we
help you?

A repair, a missing document or a question about your apartment? Include your property address so we can direct your enquiry to the right person.

info@pani-hv.de

Report a repair

Tell us where and what the problem is, and include a callback number. You can attach photos in your email app.

Request documents

Tell us which statement or document you need and the period it covers.

Urgent issues

For urgent damage to the property, such as a burst pipe or heating failure, please first use the emergency contacts provided for your building. Outside opening hours you can also reach us on 030 23125 199. Email is not an emergency channel.
