Home/About us

PANI. Personally led.

Niclas Paprocki and Paul Harm founded PANI and run the company together. With our team, we look after buildings across Berlin, today more than 500 units: from property operations and finance to construction support and energy renovation. For your property, we remain personally available.

The team

Four people. Clear responsibilities.

At PANI, every task has a name: looking after your building, the figures behind it, building works and day-to-day management. You always know who to turn to. We discuss the right level of support for your property in person.
