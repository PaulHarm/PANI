Before we start

Your move to PANI.

How do I start a change of manager?

We first discuss your property and situation. We review the existing agreements, dates and, for an owners’ association, the resolutions needed for the transition. We then agree the specific handover process with you.

What does management cost?

This depends on the property, the number of units and the agreed scope of work. After an initial discussion, we clarify the tasks and prepare a proposal tailored to them.

How long does a handover take?

This depends on the starting position, existing agreements and the availability of documents. We coordinate the handover date and individual steps with you and the previous manager.

Which documents do you need?

To get started, we need the property address, number of units and a brief outline of your situation. For the handover, we then organise the existing contracts, accounts and property records.

What happens to outstanding issues?

We record ongoing works, open concerns and missing documents during the handover. Together, we clarify priorities and responsibilities so these matters continue to be addressed.
