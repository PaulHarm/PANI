PANI. Personally led.

Niclas Paprocki and Paul Harm run PANI together. We look after buildings across Berlin, today more than 500 units. We bring property management experience and financial analysis to your property, with direct personal contact.

The founders

Two founders. Clear responsibilities.

For you, these belong together: someone looks after the building and the people in it, while income, costs and upcoming works remain clear. We discuss the right level of support for your property in person.

Niclas Paprocki

Property operations & delivery

Experience gained in his family’s property management business and through real estate studies. Close to the property, dependable in coordination and attentive to detail.

Paul Harm

Finance & strategy

Financial analysis and investment banking experience. Bringing clarity to the numbers, sound decision-making and a disciplined approach to costs.
