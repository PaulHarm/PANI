Switching to PANI.

Documents, handover, open questions: we coordinate the transition with you and your previous manager. PANI looks after buildings across Berlin and remains personally available for your property.

The process

How switching works.

01

Initial discussion

We discuss your property and situation. We then clarify the tasks and prepare a proposal tailored to them.

02

Property visit

We visit your property in person to gain our own overview of its condition, building services and particular features.

03

Document review

We review the existing agreements, dates and, for an owners’ association, the resolutions needed for the transition.

04

Handover planning

We coordinate the handover date and individual steps with you and the previous manager.

05

Start of management

We record ongoing works, open concerns and missing documents during the handover, so these matters continue to be addressed.

Before we start

Common questions about switching.

How do I start a change of manager?

With a conversation: you tell us about your property and what has prompted the change. We then go through the five steps above with you and agree what comes first in your case.

What does management cost?

This depends on the property, the number of units and the agreed scope of work. That is why we do not quote a flat fee; you receive a proposal for your property.

How long does a handover take?

This depends on the starting position, existing agreements and the availability of documents. We only give a firm timeframe once we know these three points.

Which documents do you need?

To get started, we need the property address, number of units and a brief outline of your situation. For the handover, we then organise the existing contracts, accounts and property records.

What happens to outstanding issues?

They are carried over. Together, we clarify priorities and responsibilities so that ongoing works and open concerns continue to be addressed after the handover.
