Zinshaus management in Berlin.

A Zinshaus is building fabric and capital investment in one. We manage individual buildings and entire portfolios in Berlin: with reliable day-to-day support for tenants, clear reports for your decisions and maintenance that we plan over several years.

Your Zinshaus: tenancies and building fabric.

Berlin’s residential and mixed-use buildings combine established tenancies with the needs of their building fabric. We look after both together.

  • A review of building condition, contracts and outstanding issues
  • Clear priorities for upcoming maintenance
  • Planning works around established tenancies
  • Local coordination with contractors and service providers
  • Coordination of works to the existing building fabric
  • Support for energy renovation with qualified specialists

For investors: reports as a basis for decisions.

You want to understand how your properties are performing and which decisions come next. We combine day-to-day management with reporting on income, costs and works. We agree the scope and reporting schedule around your property.

  • A clear view of property status and outstanding works
  • Consistent reporting across multiple properties
  • Reports on income, costs and property performance
  • Metrics and reporting intervals agreed with you
  • Clear records of works and decisions
  • Preparation of existing documents for a planned sale on request

See how we present figures and documents on our reporting page.

For long-term holders: planning maintenance over years.

You hold your properties for the long term. You need management that handles everyday operations and identifies future priorities at an early stage.

  • A view of costs and priorities across properties
  • Consistent processes for an established portfolio
  • Multi-year planning for maintenance and major works
  • Prioritising works by building condition and need
  • Budget planning for the agreed measures
  • Regular reviews of each property’s development

Have you inherited or taken over a building? Find out more under Succession.

How we start.

01

Take stock

We review building condition, leases, documents and open issues, for one building or for several.

02

Agree priorities and reporting

Together with you, we define which works come first, which figures you need and how often.

03

Deliver and report

We coordinate everyone involved, keep track of costs and schedules, and review progress at the agreed intervals.

All three areas of focus build on our rental building management. We agree the specific scope with you.
